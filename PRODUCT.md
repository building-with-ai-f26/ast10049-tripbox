# TripBox

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
People with many personal photos who want to choose and preserve memories from trips, life events, and everyday moments.

## Product Purpose
Help people gather photos, review suggested favorites, and create editable plans and keepsakes for sharing or printing.

## Operating Context
The course repository is the source of the GitHub Pages prototype. Users work on computers and phones. Sign-in works; cross-device style-library storage is in development and must not be represented as live until verified.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Preserve photo import, local recommendations, optional local AI, review and undo, tagging, draft planning, card studio, analytics, and privacy controls. Instagram connections, birthday workshop, account-backed preferences, and face matching remain incomplete. Birthday outputs should be prepared for user review. Protect saved data with tested account-specific access controls.

## Brand Commitments
TripBox has a warm keepsake and scrapbook feel. Retain the tagline “Camera-roll chaos, meet your happy place.” Amanda chose the visual dashboard from concept 2 and the organized workspace navigation from concept 3.

## Evidence on Hand
The live prototype and product page are in the course repository. The three generated visual concepts are design references, not evidence that the pictured features exist.

## Product Principles
- Keep the next step obvious.
- Let signed-in people revisit their style settings without repeating setup.
- Preserve user control over suggestions, drafts, and publishing.
- Explain what stays on-device and what is saved or shared.
