How your photos and data are handled
Local matching keeps trip photos in this tab. When signed in, small style-reference previews are saved to your account so you can use them on another device. The optional local AI action sends previews to OpenRouter and its model provider only with your permission. Originals are never changed.

This prototype uses PostHog for pageviews, clicks, and masked session replay. Photo areas and input values are excluded from replay.

Bringing photos from your phone?

On your phone, tap the picker and choose multiple photos from your library. On a Mac? AirDrop photos from your phone first, then choose them here. The app must be opened on your phone to use its photo library.

Export your Instagram media

  1. Open Instagram and go to your profile.
  2. Tap the menu (☰), then Accounts Center (sometimes called Meta Account).
  3. Open Your information and permissions → Export your information (or Download your information).
  4. Choose Create export, select your Instagram account, then Export to device.
  5. Under Customize information, select Media. If subcategories appear, include Posts, Stories, Reels, and Archived content where available. Story interactions is not your posted stories. You do not need messages or personal information.
  6. Choose All time, High media quality, and JSON format. Tap Start export or Create files.
  7. When Instagram notifies you, return to the export screen and download your ZIP file. On a Mac, double-click it to unzip.
  8. Back in Tripbox, choose the media JSON files and the whole unzipped export folder. You can also choose just the folder: Tripbox looks for JSON inside it.

Button names may vary. Only photos present in the export can be matched. Videos, captions, and story layouts are not analyzed by this prototype.

Create your drafts

Your labels become carousel, Story, photo-book page, and frame drafts. Change your quantities, then rebuild. Drafts keep every image’s full composition: no automatic crops. Move photos earlier or later to change their order.

These are local planning layouts, not finished print-ready books or published posts. Download originals separately. Trip projects clear on refresh. Your signed-in style library saves separately; draft plan files contain filenames and settings, not photos.
