Export your Instagram media

  1. Open Instagram and go to your profile.
  2. Tap the menu (☰), then Accounts Center (sometimes called Meta Account).
  3. Open Your information and permissions → Export your information (or Download your information).
  4. Choose Create export, select your Instagram account, then Export to device.
  5. Under Customize information, select Media. If subcategories appear, include Posts, Stories, Reels, and Archived content where available. Story interactions is not your posted stories. You do not need messages or personal information.
  6. Choose All time, High media quality, and JSON format. Tap Start export or Create files.
  7. When Instagram notifies you, return to the export screen and download your ZIP file. On a Mac, double-click it to unzip.
  8. Back in Tripbox, choose the media JSON files and the whole unzipped export folder. You can also choose just the folder: Tripbox looks for JSON inside it.

Button names may vary. Only photos present in the export can be matched. Videos, captions, and story layouts are not analyzed by this prototype.
